06 — How we build

On your property. Not on a truck.

Prefab units show up with a delivery fee and a crane. Ours are framed where you will live in them. That is the whole argument: access is simpler, the plan is more flexible, and you are not paying to fly a box over the neighbor’s fence.

07 — Construction process

Five steps. You always know which one you are in.

A project manager stays on the job once construction starts. Before that, the same team handles the estimate, the permit package, and the selections.

Free property analysis
  1. 01

    Property analysis

    An ADU expert walks the property, figures out where a tiny home can sit, and gives you an estimate.

  2. 02

    Budget review

    We sit down with the estimate, check it against your budget, and change the scope from your notes before anything is submitted.

  3. 03

    Permitting

    Once the location and floor plan are set, we package the set and submit it for the permit. You do not have to become the permit runner.

  4. 04

    Design collaboration

    While the city reviews the permit, you lock the design. Standard selections first, then any custom request you actually want.

  5. 05

    The build

    Permits approved, selections done, construction starts. You talk to the project manager directly. Status is not a mystery.
